How your team adds a new project

  1. In Squarespace, open Projects and press the + to add a post.
  2. Give it a title, add a few photos and type the site location.
  3. Tick the service it belongs to, so it also shows on that service page.
  4. Press Publish. It appears here and in Recent projects on the home page.
